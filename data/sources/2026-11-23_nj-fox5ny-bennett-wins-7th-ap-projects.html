Democrat Rebecca Bennett wins New Jersey's 7th District, AP projects

By Isabel Soisson

Democrat Rebecca Bennett beats Rep. Tom Kean Jr., wins New Jersey’s 7th congressional district race.

The Brief

    • Democrat Rebecca Bennett has won election to the U.S. House in New Jersey’s 7th Congressional District, The Associated Press projects.
    • Bennett, a former Navy helicopter pilot and health care executive, secured her first term in the U.S. House and her first elected office with her win.

NEW JERSEY - Democrat Rebecca Bennett has won election to the U.S. House in New Jersey’s 7th Congressional District, defeating Republican incumbent U.S. Rep. Tom Kean Jr., The Associated Press projects.

LIVE: Click here for real-time election results

Local perspective:

Bennett, a former Navy helicopter pilot and health care executive who won the June Democratic primary, secured her first term in the U.S. House and her first elected office with her win. She’ll now represent all of Warren and Hunterdon counties, along with portions of Sussex, Morris, Somerset and Union counties.

Dig deeper:

During the campaign, the candidates presented differing positions on the Trump administration’s policies, including its approach to Iran and presidential authority over tariffs, per the AP. During a September debate, Bennett criticized U.S. military action against Iran and called for tariff oversight, while Kean defended the administration’s approach, citing Iran’s nuclear ambitions and presidential tariff authority.

Rebecca Bennett biography 

The backstory:

Bennett, 39, lives in Bridgewater, New Jersey, with her husband and two children, and was born in New York and raised in Texas, according to her official campaign website. During her military career, she earned a master’s degree in business administration from the Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania, per her LinkedIn page.

According to her campaign website, Bennett served in the military for 15 years, and in addition to her role in the Navy, she served as an experimental test pilot and aircraft commander. She then went on to serve in the Air National Guard before settling in New Jersey with her husband, also a former Navy pilot, to raise their family.

(Photo by Kena Betancur/Getty Images)

As reported on her LinkedIn page, Bennett later worked in digital health, health care technology and pharmaceutical companies, bringing military and private-sector experience to her first campaign for elected office before defeating Kean, who had represented the seat since January 2023.

Bennett campaigned on affordability, health care access, veterans' services and government ethics. Her platform called for lowering health care costs, expanding affordable housing, cutting administrative red tape and restoring the state and local tax (SALT) deduction for New Jersey taxpayers.

She also advocated for investment in renewable energy, clean-energy manufacturing, biotechnology and health care industries in New Jersey, while addressing environmental protection, national security and support for military families.

The Source: This article includes information provided by The Associated Press.
