As of Oct. 2, 2026, 71 voting members of the U.S. Congress — 11 members of the U.S. Senate and 60 members of the U.S. House of Representatives — are not seeking re-election in 2026.

Of the 11 U.S. Senate incumbents who did not seek re-election:

  • Ten — four Democrats and six Republicans — retired from public office.
  • One Republican ran for governor.

In the previous four election cycles, there were eight retirement announcements in 2024, six in 2022, four in 2020, and three in 2018.

Of the 60 U.S. House incumbents who are not seeking re-election:

  • Thirty-two— 15 Democrats and 17 Republicans — are retiring from public office.
  • Sixteen — seven Democrats and nine Republicans — are running for the U.S. Senate.
  • Ten — all Republicans — are running for governor.
  • One Republican is running for state attorney general.
  • One Democrat is running for a state legislative district.

At this point in the last four election cycles, there were 45 retirement announcements in 2024, 49 in 2022, 36 in 2020, and 52 in 2018.

Between January 2011 and October 2026, 418 U.S. Senate and House incumbents announced they would not seek re-election. During that period, an average of 26 incumbents announced their retirement plans each year.

Of every election cycle from 2012 to 2024, the 2018 election cycle had the highest number of congressional retirements at 54, and the 2020 election cycle had the fewest at 41.

This page focuses on the U.S. Senate and House incumbents not seeking re-election in the 2026 election cycle. Ballotpedia's count of U.S. Senate incumbents not seeking re-election does not include those who are running for another office, but are not up for re-election on Nov. 3, 2026. Ballotpedia's count of U.S. House incumbents not seeking re-election does not include non-voting members of the chamber.

Neither count includes incumbents who announced their resignations, left office early, or lost re-election in primary or general elections. Click here to view information about those U.S. Senate and House incumbents.

Congressional retirements by month, 2011-2026

See also: Congressional retirements by month, 2011-2026

Between January 2011 and October 2026, 418 U.S. Congress incumbents announced they would not seek re-election.

January had the highest number of members announcing they would not run for re-election at 74. Fifty-two of those took place during election years and 22 during odd-number years. The fewest announcements took place in June at 16. In June, 13 happened during off years and three during election years.

Hover over the bars in the chart below to see the number of even- and odd-year announcements by month.

November had the second-highest total announcements at 53. Five November announcements occurred during an election year and 48 occurred during an off year.

August saw the second-fewest announcements after June at 19. In August, all but two happened during off years.
