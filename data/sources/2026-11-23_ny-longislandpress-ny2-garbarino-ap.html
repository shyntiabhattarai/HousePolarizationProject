Halpin refuses to concede CD2 race to Garbarino

By Timothy Bolger
… Comments
Posted on November 6, 2026
Pat Halpin, a former Suffolk County Executive, is hoping to flip New York’s second congressional district to the Democrats for the first time since 2010.
Photo by Pat Halpin for Congress

Former Suffolk County Executive Pat Halpin believes there he still has a chance at riding the Democratic blue wave to unseat one of Long Island’s two Republican congressmen.

U.S. Rep. Andrew Garbarino (R-Sayville) declared victory on election night, but Halpin wants to make sure every paper ballot is counted, despite the fact that the Associated Press declared the incumbent the winner in New York’s 2nd Congressional District, which runs along the South Shore from Mastic Beach to Massapequa.

“The Associated Press does not elect members of Congress. The American people do,” Halpin said in a statement. Neither representatives for the AP nor Garbarino responded to requests for comment. Neither did the boards of elections for Nassau and Suffolk counties.

The much-watched race came as Democrats unseated enough Republicans nationwide to flip control of Congress from the GOP majority amid a blue wave of voters reacting to the agenda of President Donald Trump and his Republican allies in the U.S. House and Senate. Long Island’s other three congressional representatives — two Democrats and a Republican — were all reelected, although Democrats flipped local seats in the New York State Senate, Assembly, and Town of Hempstead, with a possible upset in the Nassau County Legislature as well.

“Every time they talk about blue waves, Suffolk just pushes back,” Garbarino told cheering party faithful at the Suffolk GOP election night watch party at Stereo Garden in Patchogue on Tuesday night. “This was closer than we wanted, but we still came out on top.”

Halpin maintained on election night that the race was too close to call.

“This is much closer than any of the so-called political experts ever thought it would be,” Halpin told the Press at the Suffolk Democratic Committee election night watch party at the IBEW Local 1049 Union Hall in Holtsville. “The only victory that counts is the one that’s certified at the end of this election…. Let’s wait ’til all the votes are counted. This vote comes down to we the people.”

Halpin trails Garbarino by about 8,000 votes in the unofficial early returns tallied by the boards of election. Suffolk County Democratic Chair Rich Shaffer said Halpin came “within inches of unseating” Garbarino, but the party leader added that he does not think there are enough Democratic votes in the paper ballots for the challenger to unseat the incumbent.

Jay Jacobs, who leads the Nassau County and New York State Democratic committees, was content to let the contest play out until election works finalize the tallies in the coming days and weeks.

Jacobs told the Press, “He has every right to wait until all of the mail-in and affidavit ballots are counted.”

-With Michael Malaszczyk
